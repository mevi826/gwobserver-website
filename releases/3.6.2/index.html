Release notes

Guild Wars Observer 3.6.2

This update adds a Meta tab that shows how skills are used across recorded matches. It also corrects the energy cost of cancelled skills and fixes a few Library and replay issues.

Meta

A new Meta tab shows how skills are used across every recorded match.

The skill map

  • Explore a 3D map of every skill, grouped by profession.
    • High icon: teams with this skill often won.
    • Big icon: many players used this skill.
  • Hold the right mouse button and drag to turn the view, and use the wheel to zoom.
  • Find a skill by typing its name, or press the arrow beside the search field to browse every recorded skill, as in the Library.
  • Use the “All recorded matches” list to choose a period:
    • All recorded matches: every match.
    • A month: only the matches of that month.
    • A balance era: only the matches between two balance updates.

A skill up close

Click a skill on the map, or pick it in the table, to see:

  • the skills usually on the same bar, linked on the map and listed beside it;
  • its usage over time and its balance history;
  • the most played bars with it. Open any listed match directly in the replay.

Fluxes, time-lapse and balance updates

  • Filter by flux to see how it changes the meta, compared with the other fluxes.
  • Press Time-lapse to watch the map change month by month, with balance patches marked along the way.
  • Select a balance update to see what it changed and which skills actually rose or fell afterwards.

The table

  • Switch to the table. For each skill it shows:
    • Players: the percentage of players who used the skill.
    • Usage trend: how its use changed over time.
    • Team wins: how often teams with this skill won.
    • Team games: how many matches this win rate comes from.
  • Filter by period, balance update or profession. Show rare skills adds the skills used on only a few bars.

Win rates only count matches where one team used the skill and the other didn’t. Guardian, for example, is on almost every team, so most matches have it on both sides and are left out: its win rate comes from the few hundred matches where only one team brought it, and the Team games column shows that number. They don’t measure how strong a skill is. Skills mostly used after a death, such as Resurrection Signet, carry a warning.

Energy estimates

  • Fixed cancelled skills giving their Energy back. A cancelled skill now keeps its cost, like an interrupted one, and “Energy spent” includes it. Henchmen now also pay for cancelled and interrupted skills.
  • Added “Energy lost to cancelled casts” to the Match Library stats.

Other changes

  • Selecting a match in the Library no longer downloads it. The Overview tab only reads matches already on your computer, so attributes appear once the match is downloaded. The Stats and Map tabs and Replay still download it.
  • The replay now remembers your volume, mute and Sound FX panel settings between matches and after you restart the app.
  • In the Piano Roll, you can now hide the spike card and the Monk cards. Click the thin bar beside the spike card or above the Monk cards. The roll then uses the free space, and the app remembers your choice.
