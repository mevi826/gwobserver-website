Release notes

Guild Wars Observer 3.5.1

Update 3.5.1 is a patch release. It brings a camera that behaves like the in-game camera, map effects on the Isle of Meditation and the Isle of the Weeping Stone, better health and energy estimates, and a set of rendering fixes.

Download 3.5.1 Back to gwobserver.com

What’s new

Camera

Reworked camera control and transparency to behave as in game when the camera is focused on a player: it is no longer possible to pan vertically into the ground, and transparency is applied to props in the way so the character stays visible.

Free camera still allows you to move freely around the map and terrain.

Guild Hall Maps

Added Miasma texture on Isle of Meditation.

Added Stone Spores texture and effect on Isle of the Weeping Stone.

Added a gate closed/open indicator on Isle of Meditation (same as existing ones, e.g. Frozen Isle).

Health and Energy Estimates

Added Vital Weapon to the maximum health estimate (its bonus was read as extra armour before).

Added Spirit Siphon energy gain: recorded amount when available, otherwise estimated from the nearest spirit (explained in the energy lamp tooltip).

Other fixes

  • Fixed the follow camera dropping a player who died for good late in a match.
  • Clicking a player on the map now only follows them, like clicking their row in the party window. Player info opens from the right-click menu.
  • Fixed the bamboo gate on Isle of Jade tearing apart when it opens.
  • Fixed white spots on stone props on Uncharted Isle, and the shine of the Isle of Solitude banners.
  • Fixed players standing in water being cut off at the waterline.
  • Fixed the water level on Isle of the Dead.
  • Fixed the teleporter rings on Isle of Jade, Isle of Solitude and Nomad’s Isle hiding players standing inside them.
  • Fixed a carried flag sometimes showing the other team’s colour.
  • Added Sprint, Rush and Frenzy effects on the character, and animated textures on a few skill effects.
