Release notes

Guild Wars Observer 3.5.0

Update 3.5.0 adds skill particle effects and more game sounds to replays, GIF export, and improvements to the replay interface.

Recording compatibility: Skill landing effects and lasting visuals (hexes, enchantments, conditions) require recordings made from 7 October 2026 onward. Older recordings still support projectiles, healing and damage bursts, cast and area effects, and sounds.

Download 3.5.0 Back to gwobserver.com

We’re super excited to share this update with you. Bringing skill effects into replays has been a goal since we started GW Observer, but for a long time we weren’t sure we could make it work. It turned out to be the hardest part of the reverse engineering, with plenty of trial and error along the way. Seeing those effects play back in a match has been a special moment for us, and we’re glad you can finally try it yourselves.

With this update, replays are now as close as we can get to the in-game observer experience. This means we’re nearing the end of the reverse engineering work, so the next updates will focus more on bug fixes, additional statistics and UI improvements. Don’t hesitate to share your feedback, it will help us decide what to work on next.

What’s new

Skill Effects

Projectiles, casting effects, healing bursts, hexes, enchantments, conditions, wards and wells now appear during replays. Each category can be toggled in Preferences.

Game Sounds

Almost half of the recorded sounds were previously silent. Replays now play them, along with more attack, impact and skill sounds.

GIF Export Alt+X

Use the new Clip button or Alt+X to capture and share part of a replay.

You are free to move and resize the capture window, and to change the frame rate and output size so the GIF fits within Discord’s 10 MB limit. If you want, you can also remove the limit and export much bigger GIFs.

Replay Interface

Added support for Echo, Arcane Echo and Keystone Signet, with fixes to skill disables, effect tracking and overhead numbers. Miasma on the Isle of Meditation now appears as Miasma instead of Disease.

We heavily reworked the rules that attribute damage and healing to players and skills, which drive the overhead numbers and skill icons in the replay window. We also refined the rules for attribute calculation and energy estimation.

Vampiric attacks now show the game’s Life Draining icon beside their damage and healing numbers.

Split Camera

Simpler player selection and an optional HUD for the watched player.

Guild Hall Details

Henchmen now appear in the party window and are rendered in 3D with their models. On Warrior’s Isle, a catapult indicator shows the danger zone where the projectile will land.

We also added 36 effect bar rules, 54 attribute estimation rules and 15 energy estimation rules. These should improve coverage of niche builds too, though estimation errors can still occur. We keep monitoring new games to refine these rules.

Other fixes

  • Skill icon and laser settings are saved between sessions.
  • Music resumes at the right point after seeking.
  • Left-drag panning no longer lets the cursor drift.

Known limitations

Energy remains an estimate, and adrenaline is not recorded, so adrenaline skills still appear charged.

Thanks to everyone who has tested GW Observer and reported issues. The more people share suggestions and report bugs, the better we can make the tool.
