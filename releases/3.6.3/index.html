Release notes

Guild Wars Observer 3.6.3

Share a match replay with a link that opens GW Observer at the exact moment you choose, and add separately recorded voice comms to your replays.

Download 3.6.3 Back to gwobserver.com

In this update

  1. Share links
  2. Voice comms
  3. Other improvements

Share links

  • Press the Share button on the play bar to create a link to a published match at a specific moment. Copy the link and share it anywhere, for example on Discord: whoever opens it watches the match from that exact moment.
    • The Share menu copies a link to this moment or from the match start, or makes a GIF clip.
  • Links open the match in GW Observer and download it automatically if needed. You can also paste a link into the Library.

Voice comms

  • Add your team’s voice comms to a replay: drag and drop any audio file, then sync it with the match using the Sync editor.
    • Drag the comms clip until the recording and the match replay are in sync.
  • Adjust playback with the Comms waveform and Game / Comms volume mixer. Recordings are remembered per match on your PC and never uploaded to the public match library.
  • The Library now groups ratings, notes and comms indicators in a Notes column, with filters for rated matches, matches with notes and matches with voice comms.

Other improvements

  • Flags, vine seeds and repair kits now appear as 3D models on the ground. You can select them and focus the camera on them.
  • GIF clips now use the replay’s current playback speed.
  • Fixed energy tracking for Lyssa’s Aura and Second Wind: energy gained from these skills is now calculated correctly.
  • Power Block, Cry of Frustration and Power Leech were missing from the interrupt stats. They are now counted.
  • Teinai’s Wind and Lyssa’s Haste can interrupt several enemies at once. The stats now count a separate interrupt attempt for each enemy in range who was using a skill.
  • Rejuvenation: the spirit loses Health each time it heals a party member. The number over the spirit now shows that loss correctly, the same amount as its heal (for example -9), instead of a wrong value.
  • Fixed the app opening off-screen after restoring a saved window position. It now opens in the centre of the screen if that position is no longer visible.

Thanks to everyone who tested the update and reported problems. Keep sending us your feedback!
